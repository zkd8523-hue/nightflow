# Notes — where-to-stay-in-seoul-for-nightlife (researched 2026-10-04)

Every fact in the post maps to one line here. [NF] = NightFlow page fetched 2026-10-04.

## Club scenes (NightFlow)
- Hongdae: hip-hop, K-pop, EDM; youngest crowd (20s–30s recommended); entry usually ₩10,000–30,000, free for women at many venues; most clubs take walk-ins, "almost universally open-door" (quote from thedj-diaries on page); popular clubs queue after 23:00 Fri/Sat; good for solo; "first night in Korea". [NF /en/clubs/hongdae]
- Itaewon: most international district; house, EDM, disco, R&B, hip-hop; techno/house heads (Cakeshop, Faust, Soap named); most staff speak English; mixed ages 20s–40s; good solo (Western-style bars, can sit alone); bottle tables from ₩500k; many clubs Fri–Sat only, quiet mid-week. [NF /en/clubs/itaewon]
- Gangnam: upscale; big EDM clubs, hip-hop, VIP lounges in Apgujeong and Cheongdam; tables ₩750,000+ main / ₩1,500,000+ VIP; strict dress (no shorts, sandals, athletic wear); fits groups of 4–10 with a table, 25–40s; skip if solo man without a table or weekend walk-in without table. Club locations named: Club ACE (Sinsa), Core Lounge (Apgujeong), DM Seoul (Apgujeong), Arzu (Cheongdam). [NF /en/clubs/gangnam]
- Hours: most Seoul clubs 22:00–05:00; empty before midnight; fills around 1 a.m. [NF /en/club-hours]
- Halloween 2026 = Saturday Oct 31; NF Hongdae/Itaewon pages say clubs hit capacity by 22:00 that night. [NF /en/clubs/hongdae, /en/clubs/itaewon]
- Passport (physical) required, 19+ (born 2007 or earlier in 2026). [NF area pages]

## Stations (Wikipedia station articles, fetched 2026-10-04)
- Hongik University station: Line 2, AREX, Gyeongui–Jungang Line.
- Sangsu station: Line 6; also serves Hongdae.
- Itaewon station: Line 6; many shops/restaurants for foreigners close to station.
- Sinsa station: Line 3 + Shinbundang Line. Apgujeong station: Line 3. Apgujeongrodeo station: Suin–Bundang Line. Cheongdam station: Line 7. Gangnam station: Line 2.
- AREX runs Incheon Airport ↔ Seoul Station via Hongik University (Wikipedia AREX; search result showed direct trains Hongik Univ ↔ Incheon T1/T2).

## Daytime (official tourism sites)
- Hongdae: youthful area, clothing/cosmetics/book shops, cafés, buskers and dance performances; has a "Club Street". VisitKorea https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=86853
- Hongdae route: street music on walking street until 22:00, Yeonnam park cafés. [NF /en/night-activities/night-routes]
- Itaewon: Seoul's first special tourist zone (1997); multicultural, over 20,000 foreign residents; ~2,000 shops. VisitKorea https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=111543
- Itaewon: World Food Street dinner; Haebangchon rooftop N Seoul Tower view. [NF night-routes]
- Itaewon Tourist Information Center inside Itaewon Station. Visit Seoul https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Itaewon-Tourist-Information-Center-Itaewon-Station_/27686
- Gangnam: Garosu-gil in Sinsa-dong (fashion, cafés), Apgujeong Rodeo Street (luxury brands, multi-shops); Starfield Library in COEX Mall. VisitKorea vcontsId=111880; Visit Seoul Starfield Library ENP026558

## Getting back
- Seoul subway: since 2022, weekdays run to 1 a.m. (at terminal station); Saturdays, Sundays and holidays stay at midnight. Dailian 2022-05-30 https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1118182 ; Seoul Shinmun 2022-05-29 (weekday 1 a.m., terminal basis). => Friday night counts as weekday. NF night-routes: "Saturday last trains end about an hour earlier than weekdays."
- NF getting-around: last trains around midnight, check app on the day; set up taxi app; Kakao T reportedly works with foreign cards (travel guides); taxis cost more 22:00–04:00 (VisitKorea).
- Seoul taxi surcharge (regular taxi, from Feb 1 2023): 20% 22–23h, 40% 23–02h, 20% 02–04h; base ₩4,800 day. Seoul Metropolitan Government https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/modes-of-transport/taxi/
- Owl buses: late-night buses, numbers start with N. Seoul Metropolitan Government https://english.seoul.go.kr/travel-safely-around-city-even-night-seouls-owl-bus/ (2016 page; route count not used)
- Help numbers 1330 / 112 / 119. [NF getting-around]
