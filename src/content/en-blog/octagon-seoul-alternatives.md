Club Octagon is most likely closed. Korean references list it as shut since 2020, and we found no dated news of a reopening (last checked 4 October 2026). If you want the kind of night it was known for, big-sound electronic music in Gangnam, there are clubs open now that play EDM, house and techno, and several of them take table bookings.

## Is Club Octagon open in 2026?

Here is exactly what we could confirm as of October 2026:

- **No closure notice from news or the club itself.** We searched English and Korean news for a dated report of Octagon closing or reopening and did not find one.
- **References list it as closed.** Korean wiki entries and other Seoul club sites describe Octagon as shut since 2020, during the COVID-19 period. These are not news sources, so treat the date as unconfirmed.
- **No reopening news either.** Talk of a revival under the same name has circulated, but we found no dated report that it happened.
- **Promoter pages still exist.** Some promoter sites still advertise "Octagon" guest lists and tables. We could not match any of them to a dated reopening announcement.

So the honest answer: assume Octagon is closed unless you see recent, dated proof that it is open.

## What Club Octagon was known for

Octagon opened in 2011 in Nonhyeon-dong, Gangnam, in the basement of a hotel. A Korean newspaper described it in April 2019 as the largest club in the country, with room for about 3,000 people at once, and known for bringing in well-known DJs.

It was a regular in DJ Mag's Top 100 Clubs poll in the 2010s. Musically it was an electronic club, EDM and house on a big sound system. That is the night most people searching for it are after.

### Closest to the old Gangnam EDM night

**Core Seoul** is tagged lounge, EDM and techno, and opens Friday and Saturday from 22:00 to 05:00. Its page lists a ₩10,000 entry fee and notes entry may be limited when it is full.

**Lion Super Club** is a club-format EDM venue. It opens Thursdays from 22:00 and Fridays and Saturdays from 23:00. Entry is listed at ₩25,000 in advance or ₩30,000 at the door, with two drinks included.

**Club Ace**, near Sinsa, mixes EDM and hip-hop and opens Tuesday to Sunday from 23:00. Its page lists entry at ₩20,000 for men and free for women.

### If you lean more house and techno

**Frame Seoul** (techno and EDM, 23:30 to 07:00) and **MUSE SEOUL** (house and techno, Friday and Saturday 22:00 to 05:00) are both in Gangnam. For both, plan on walking in and paying at the door (MUSE lists ₩10,000; Frame's fee varies by event).

Outside Gangnam, **Cakeshop** in Itaewon is tagged house and techno and opens Thursday to Saturday from 22:00. Entry is listed at ₩15,000 to ₩20,000 depending on the party.

### If you want EDM in Hongdae

**OCEAN** in Hongdae is an EDM club that opens daily from 22:00 until 06:00, and until 07:00 on weekends. Hongdae is more casual and cheaper than Gangnam. See the full list on the [Hongdae clubs page](/en/clubs/hongdae).

## Tables, prices and the door in Gangnam

Gangnam is where a table matters most. On NightFlow, a Gangnam table has a minimum spend of ₩1,000,000 for the whole group, filled from the club's own drinks menu. At Core Seoul the cheapest set is ₩450,000, and at Lion Super Club and Club Ace the cheapest bottle is ₩350,000. The [VIP tables page](/en/vip-tables) shows each club's menu prices.

Of the clubs above, Core Seoul, Core Lounge, Lion Super Club, Club Ace and OCEAN can be booked through NightFlow in English; you pay the club on the night.

Dress up a little. Gangnam is the strictest district in Seoul: shorts, slippers and sweatpants can get men turned away. Details are on the [dress code page](/en/dress-code).

## When to go

Most Seoul clubs open around 22:00 and close around 05:00, but rooms rarely fill before 1am. If you have a table, arriving around midnight works well. Friday and Saturday are the big nights in Gangnam; several clubs above open only on weekends. Check [opening hours](/en/club-hours) before you head out.
