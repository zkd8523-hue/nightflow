## Club scene (NightFlow)
- Busan scene concentrated in Haeundae (beach-front clubs, summer peak, hotel clubs with table culture) and Seomyeon (year-round downtown). More relaxed than Seoul. Entry ₩15,000–30,000. Crowd mostly local, some tourists in summer beach season. Tables min spend ₩500,000. [NF /en/clubs/busan]
- Seomyeon = Busan's club street; bookable: Groove & Spot (open nightly, hip-hop & EDM, buy 1 drink to enter), BELPOS (Fri–Sat, no slippers), Azit (Thu–Sun from ~1 a.m., entry ₩20,000). Most clubs Fri–Sat until early morning; Sun–Thu suggestion = Haeundae evening. [NF /en/night-activities/busan-night]
- Gwangalli drone show: free, ~10–12 min, every Saturday twice; Oct–Feb 19:00 & 21:00, Mar–Sep 20:00 & 22:00; weather can cancel. Gwangan station Line 2. [NF busan-night]
- No clubs listed in Gwangalli on NightFlow.
- Haeundae evening: Blue Line Park beach train ₩8,000–10,000/ride (official), runs until about 19:30 in October; Haeundae Market street food; The Bay 101 / Marine City skyline after dark. [NF /en/night-activities/haeundae-evening]
- Passport (physical), 19+ (born 2007 or earlier in 2026); carry ₩20,000–30,000 cash since some door terminals refuse foreign cards. [NF clubs/busan]

## Stations & distances
- Seomyeon station: Busan Metro Lines 1 and 2 (Wikipedia; Visit Busan "Exit 2 of Busan Metro Lines 1 and 2" for Samjung Tower).
- Haeundae station: Line 2. Haeundae Beach: exit 3 or 5, ~5 min walk. Busan city https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/beaches/1325815
- Gwangalli Beach: 13-minute walk from Exit 5 of Gwangan Station, Line 2. Visit Busan https://www.visitbusan.net/en/index.do?menuCd=DOM_000000301001001000&uc_seq=377&lang_cd=en
- Line 2 station numbers (Wikipedia Busan Metro Line 2): Seomyeon 219 (km 16.3), Geumnyeonsan 210, Gwangan 209 (km 7.7), Haeundae 203 (km 1.8). => Seomyeon–Gwangan 10 stops, Seomyeon–Haeundae 16 stops, Gwangan–Haeundae 6 stops.
- Airport: Gimhae airport light rail, transfer at Sasang (Line 2) or Daejeo (Line 3) — best/cheapest to downtown. Visit Busan "Preparing for a Trip" https://www.visitbusan.net/en/index.do?menuCd=DOM_000000304011003000

## Daytime (Visit Busan)
- Seomyeon: largest downtown, shopping, crowded day and night; Samjung Tower; Seomyeon Market food alley; Jeonpo Cafe Street. (uc_seq=460, uc_seq=328 search results)
- Gwangalli: closest beach to city centre, trendy meeting place for young locals; restaurants, sea-view cafés; Gwangandaegyo Bridge lit at night; marine sports (paddleboarding, windsurfing); Busan International Fireworks Festival held here yearly. (uc_seq=377)
- Haeundae: most popular, best-known beach in Korea; tourist info center, showers. Busan city page.

## Getting back
- Busan Metro operates 05:00–23:59; first/last trains vary by station. "There is no separate night bus." Buses: extra charge after 23:40. Visit Busan Preparing for a Trip (above).
- Busan taxi late-night surcharge (from June 1, 2023): 23:00–04:00; 30% 00:00–02:00, 20% other hours; base fare ₩4,800. Kyunghyang 2023-05-14 https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202305141045011
- NF getting-around: set up taxi app; Kakao T reportedly works with foreign cards; 1330 helpline.
