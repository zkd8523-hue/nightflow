# Research notes — going-out-alone-in-seoul (2026-10-04)

All facts used in the post, with source. Paraphrased, not copied.

## NightFlow pages (fetched 2026-10-04)

### /en/clubs/hongdae
- Youngest crowd in Seoul, mostly 20s–30s, many international visitors and college students.
- Music: hip-hop, K-pop, EDM.
- Door: almost always open; pay cover and walk in. Popular venues may cap capacity Fri/Sat after 23:00.
- Cover ₩10,000–30,000 (often waived for women). Drinks ₩10,000–15,000. Good night possible under ₩70,000.
- Described as one of Seoul's easiest districts for going out solo; mixed, international crowds; free-entry options.
- Standing-room venues, loud — not for quiet drinking.

### /en/clubs/itaewon
- Crowd mid-20s to 40s, roughly half Korean, half international; most diverse in Seoul (locals, expats, tourists).
- Music: house, EDM, disco, R&B, hip-hop; underground techno (Cakeshop).
- Cover ₩30,000–40,000. Welcoming door; doors rarely turn foreigners away for being foreign.
- Many clubs open Fri/Sat only; several closed or quiet midweek.
- Western-style bars where you can sit alone with a drink; one of the easiest districts to go out solo.
- Most accept cards; carry ₩20,000–30,000 cash as backup.

### /en/clubs/gangnam
- Recommended age 25–40s. Tables of 4–10 work best; solo men face challenges. Solo discouraged; Hongdae/Itaewon better.
- Music: EDM, hip-hop, bottle service.
- Table booking essentially required; walk-ins without reservations may be refused, esp. weekends. Tables from ₩750,000+.
- Strict dress: no shorts, sandals, tank tops, athletic wear.
- Queues after 23:00 Fri–Sat; may stop new entries at capacity; arrive before 23:00 for walk-ins.

### /en/club-entry-rules
- Physical passport or ARC; photocopies/photos not accepted; almost no flexibility (legal obligation).
- Year-based age: 2026 → born 2007 or earlier, regardless of birthday. Some clubs set stricter minimums (e.g., 23+).
- Clubs can refuse at their discretion (e.g., all-male groups).
- Solo guests allowed; some Hongdae venues welcome it; solo men may face stricter doors at some places.
- Pre-booked table settles the door question in advance.
- Carry ₩20,000–30,000 cash; no slippers/flip-flops.

### /en/dress-code
- Everywhere: no slippers, no flip-flops; shoes judged first.
- Gangnam strictest (shorts, slippers, sweatpants can get men turned away); safe = shirt or clean knit + dress shoes or clean sneakers.
- Hongdae most relaxed: jeans + sneakers fine.
- Itaewon middle; wide range OK; avoid casual footwear.

### /en/guests
- Weekly guest deals from club MDs: free entry, discounted cover, free drink tickets. Gangnam, Hongdae, Itaewon. Updated Mondays.
- How: pick deal on NightFlow, copy prewritten English message, DM the MD on Instagram, get confirmation, give name at door.
- Usually free entry until a set time (often 12 AM), discount after. No Korean needed.
- No group-size restriction mentioned.

### /en/gangnam-booking-club
- "Booking": Gangnam custom where table staff (waiter/MD) bring guests from other tables over for a few minutes if your group is open to it. Optional; anyone can decline.
- Needs a table; people at the bar alone cannot take part.
- Nobody is paid to sit at the table; no hostesses; venue earns from table minimums and bottles. Tipping not expected.
- More common in lounges/hip-hop venues than big EDM clubs.

### /en/night-activities/getting-around
- Last trains around midnight; varies by line, station, day — check subway app on the day.
- Kakao T; surge pricing 22:00–04:00. Carry some cash.
- 1330 travel helpline (interpretation, tourist police link), 112 police, 119 emergency.
- Plan your ride home before the night starts.

## External official sources

- VisitKorea "Ride-hailing Apps in Korea" (updated Oct 2025): https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=197907
  - Kakao T needs a Kakao Talk account; k.ride is foreigner-only, no Kakao Talk needed, accepts international card registration, English/Japanese/Chinese.
  - TABA: foreigner-focused, sign up with mobile number or Google/Apple ID, automatic card payment.
  - TADA accepts international mobile number and credit card.
- Visit Seoul "Smart & Safe Seoul Travel": https://english.visitseoul.net/safe-travel — TABA backed by Seoul Metropolitan Government; 1330 for tourism inquiries.
