## 1. Dcard (대만) — 「旅遊」 또는 「韓國」 게시판

**標題：** 首爾夜店包廂怎麼訂？低消、被卡、要不要找公關（12 月聖誕跨年前整理）

**內文：**
最近幫幾團朋友訂首爾夜店，整理一下大家最常問的：

1. 包廂（韓國叫 테이블）不是獨立房間，是店裡的桌位。低消按桌算，整團分攤。
   - 江南大型夜店：每桌低消約 100 萬韓元 → 8 個人一人約 12.5 萬
   - 弘大、梨泰院：每桌約 50 萬韓元 → 8 個人一人約 6.25 萬
2. 最怕的「被卡」：江南門口最嚴，短褲、背心、拖鞋直接不行；要帶護照正本，19 歲以上（2026 年是 2007 年含以前出生）。有包廂一樣要符合這些規定。
3. 平安夜（12/24 週四）跟跨年（12/31 週四）是最滿的晚上，23 點後排隊很長，人多的團建議提前 2–3 週訂。
4. 要不要找公關：可以，但價格常常不透明。重點是確認「酒單上的價格」和「最後總額」有沒有先寫給你。
5. 回程：地鐵 0 點左右收班，假日晚上 2 點後很難叫車，很多人待到首班車。

我自己是用 NightFlow（nightflow.kr/zh-tw）訂的，中文表單、免訂金、當晚在店裡付款，有興趣可以參考。有問題留言我盡量回。

**Title:** Booking a club table in Seoul for a group — what it actually costs (and what gets you turned away)

**Body:**
I run a small table-booking service in Seoul, so I field the same questions a lot. Here's the honest version, no links:

- A "table" is your seat in the club, not a private room. The minimum spend is per table and the group splits it.
  - Gangnam big clubs: about ₩1,000,000 per table → ~₩125,000 each for 8 people
  - Hongdae / Itaewon: about ₩500,000 per table → ~₩62,500 each for 8
- You fill the minimum from the club's own menu. Ask for the menu prices and the final total in writing before the night.
- Doors: 19+ (in 2026, born 2007 or earlier), physical passport, and Gangnam is strict on dress — no shorts, tank tops or slides. A table doesn't override those.
- Christmas Eve (Thu Dec 24) and New Year's Eve (Thu Dec 31) are the busiest nights; queues get long after 23:00. Groups should book 2–3 weeks out.
- Getting home: subway stops around midnight, taxis are scarce after 2am on holiday nights, many people stay until the first train.
